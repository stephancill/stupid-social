Current, source-of-truth listing copy for the App Store version. Feed back to ASC via
`populate_listing.py`. Keep fields current.

The framing is "only the best parts of social": lead with what the app brings together (your chats, notifications and stories from every app, in one place), not with what it leaves out. "No feed" and "no algorithm" are at most a quiet aside, never the hook.

## Description

```
only the best parts of social: your chats, notifications and stories from all the apps you care about, in one place.

what's in it
• one inbox for your whatsapp, telegram, instagram, x, discord, linkedin, bluesky, spotify and apron chats
• photos, videos, voice notes, stickers, files, replies and reactions, with voice notes transcribed on your phone
• every notification in one list: mentions, replies, likes and follows from x, instagram, bluesky, farcaster, linkedin and github
• stories from all your favourite apps in one row: instagram stories, spotify listening activity and github activity
• the post behind a notification, so you can like, reply or repost right there
• reminders on messages you want to get back to
• search for people across networks
• as many accounts per network as you like, synced across your devices with icloud keychain

privacy
• your logins live in your icloud keychain
• the app counts anonymous usage and crashes, never your messages, names or accounts
```

## Notes / caveats for App Review

- The app shows only notifications/stories chosen by the connected accounts; it has no algorithmic feed and no user-content browsing catalog.
- Account connections require the user's own login to the corresponding provider (X requirements, Instagram, Farcaster handles, Bluesky OAuth, Spotify, GitHub). Credentials are stored in iCloud Keychain.
- Networking fetches provider data over HTTPS only.
- Export compliance: `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` is set in Info.plist.
- Preview content for review ships in the release build: tap the Settings About heading four times, then Demo Data → Preview content (see `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` § Preview / demo mode).
